Getting started on the internet

You might be surprised at how much you're able to do on the internet – from connecting with friends and family, carrying out tasks like shopping and banking, and accessing services such as your GP surgery. The internet can also be a great source of entertainment and education.

Why get online?

Being online can help you stay in touch with the people who matter most, whether that's a video call with family or catching up with friends on social media.

What do I need to get started?

You'll need a device such as a smartphone, tablet, laptop or computer, and an internet connection. Many libraries and community centres also offer free Wi-Fi and computer access.

Getting support

If you'd like one-to-one help getting online, our Digital Champions can support you at your own pace. Contact your local Age UK to find out what's available in your area.
