Visitor parking permits

A visitor parking permit is a temporary permit for visitors to park near your home.

Who can buy a visitor parking permit

To buy a visitor parking permit, you must:

  • be 16 or over
  • live in Camden for at least five nights per week

Buy a visitor parking permit

You can buy visitor permits online by making a parking account in the Camden Account.

Visitor permits:

  • can be bought on the day or up to 20 days in advance
  • cannot be refunded once you've bought them
  • cannot be changed once you've bought them

How to apply for a visitor parking permit
